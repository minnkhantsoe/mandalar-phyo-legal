About Us

Khaing Mandalar Phyo is a bus ticket booking app that lets passengers search routes, pick seats, book tickets, and upload payment confirmation — all from their phone, without needing to visit a ticket counter in person.

What the App Does

  • Browse available bus routes and departments
  • Pick a seat from a live seat layout
  • Book a ticket and upload proof of payment
  • Track booking status and get notified when it's approved
  • View past bookings and ticket history
