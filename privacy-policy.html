Privacy Policy

Khaing Mandalar Phyo (bus ticket booking app) — Last updated: September 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the Khaing Mandalar Phyo mobile app ("the App") collects, how it is used, and your choices. By using the App, you agree to this policy.

Information We Collect

We collect the following information when you use the App:

  • Account information: name, phone number, and optionally email address and NRC (national ID) number, used to create your account and identify you for bookings.
  • Booking information: travel routes, dates, seat selections, and ticket/passenger details you enter when booking a trip.
  • Payment screenshots: images you upload as proof of payment for a ticket. We do not collect or process card numbers or bank credentials directly — payment is made outside the App and confirmed via the screenshot you upload.
  • Device and notification data: a push notification token, used to send you booking status updates (e.g. payment approved/rejected, trip reminders).
  • Login session data: an authentication token stored securely on your device so you stay logged in between sessions.

How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account and bookings
  • To verify payment and confirm or reject tickets
  • To send you notifications about your bookings
  • To provide customer support

Who We Share Data With

We share booking and passenger details with the relevant bus department/operator solely to fulfill your ticket. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with advertisers or data brokers.

Data Retention

We retain account and booking data for as long as your account is active, or as needed to comply with legal or accounting obligations. You can request deletion of your account and associated data at any time (see Contact below).

Your Choices

  • You can edit or update your profile information within the App.
  • You can disable push notifications from your device's notification settings.
  • You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data by contacting us.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Security

We use industry-standard measures (including secure, encrypted local storage for session tokens) to protect your information. However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
